# Instrucciones para podcast diario · 2026-08-07

Eres un productor de podcast especializado en fotografía. Tu tarea es:

1. **Leer** el texto completo de cada artículo a continuación.
2. **Resumir** cada artículo en 2-3 frases en español, destacando la inspiración fotográfica.
3. **Redactar un texto locutable** en español, con tono natural y cercano, como para un programa de radio.
4. **Generar el audio** del texto locutable: solo voz, sin música, sin efectos de sonido.

Formato del resultado final (para enviar por correo):
  - Un texto en español con los resúmenes de todos los artículos del día.
  - Un archivo de audio con la locución de ese texto.

A continuación tienes el texto completo de cada artículo. Léelos todos
y a partir de ahí genera el resumen locutable. No te saltes ningún artículo.

**Stage Photographer Martinus Ragita Dives into the World of Lomography**
Fotógrafos: adi_totp

Music is visual.Martinus Ragita is a professional Indonesian stage and concert photographer who translates the raw energy, rhythm, and atmosphere of live performances into timeless frames. He serves as the primary photographer for several of Indonesia's biggest contemporary music acts, including .Feast, Hindia, Lomba Sihir, and breakout pop star Bernadya. We sent two rolls of the LomoChrome Purple Pétillant 35 mm ISO 100–400 and Berlin Kino B&W 35 mm ISO 400. He wasted no time and dove straight into the world of Lomography. © Martinus Ragita using LomoChrome Purple Pétillant 35 mm ISO 100–400 Hi, Martinus! It’s great to have you here at Lomography! Tell us about yourself. What do you do? What are some of your interests? Hi Lomography, happy to be here! I’m a photographer based in Jakarta, mostly focused on music/stage photography, though I love jumping between portrait, couple, and street photography, too – honestly, anything that catches my attention, especially people, as subjects. © Martinus Ragita using LomoChrome Purple Pétillant 35 mm ISO 100–400 What made you start taking photos? At first, it was just for fun back in elementary school when I borrowed my relative’s camera. It slowly became a hobby throughout middle and high school, then turned into my passion and work during college. I just really love capturing moments that will never happen again, and knowing people can hold onto those memories through my photos makes me happy. © Martinus Ragita using LomoChrome Purple Pétillant 35 mm ISO 100–400 What's your favorite thing about analogue photography? That I can’t really recreate the same character and effects with a digital camera. I also love the whole process – from taking the photo to finally seeing the results. It feels exciting every time, like a small adrenaline rush. “I like LomoChrome Purple! It gives such a funky and eclectic feel without needing much editing or color grading.” How do you describe your photography? Do you have any film photographers that you look up to? I don’t think I have one specific style, but my photos have always been about people and moments that not everyone notices. I also love experimenting with colors and lighting whenever I get the chance. As for inspirations, I look up to Martin Parr and Trevor Roberts a lot. © Martinus Ragita using LomoChrome Purple Pétillant 35 mm ISO 100–400 What's it like to be Martinus these days? How do you spend your week? On weekdays, wake up early, go to the gym, run, sometimes play pickleball with friends, and spend my time editing photos. On weekends, I mostly travel and work with musicians during their concerts, so it feels like working and traveling at the same time. © Martinus Ragita using LomoChrome Purple Pétillant 35 mm ISO 100–400 What is it like to be a photographer in Jakarta? Please tell us your experience. Jakarta is hectic, loud, and fast-paced, so being a photographer here can get pretty tiring. But the city is full of energy, and there’s always easy access to events, communities, and creative opportunities. “...love the whole process – from taking the photo to finally seeing the results. It feels exciting every time, like a small adrenaline rush.” What did you decide to shoot with the LomoChrome Purple? I used it to experiment with some shots during Lomba Sihir’s stage at the Bla Bla Bla Fest in Pontianak. © Martinus Ragita using LomoChrome Purple Pétillant 35 mm ISO 100–400 What did you like the most about this film stock? It really stands out. I rarely see colors like these in other films. It gives such a funky and eclectic feel without needing much editing or color grading. © Martinus Ragita using LomoChrome Purple Pétillant 35 mm ISO 100–400 Was there one photo from this shoot that stood out to you? What makes it special? I got a photo of Enrico, Lomba Sihir's drummer. He was about to eat, but somehow he fell asleep. It was a really unusual and funny moment, and that's why the photo stands out to me. © Martinus Ragita using LomoChrome Purple Pétillant 35 mm ISO 100–400 And how about the Berlin Kino B&W 35 mm ISO 400? Was it your first time using this film? Yes, it was my first time using it, and I really liked it. It’s not your usual black-and-white film. It has more texture and sharpness, which makes the photos feel more alive. © Martinus Ragita using Berlin Kino B&W 35 mm ISO 400 If this photo series had a soundtrack, which song would you choose to capture its spirit? Probably something from Bleachers, You and Forever. © Martinus Ragita using Berlin Kino B&W 35 mm ISO 400 What is one thing you have been dying to explore further (anything from a place to a particular subject)? What's your dream project? I’d love to shoot an international music festival or concert someday. One of my biggest dreams is to photograph my favorite artists live on stage. “...I just really love capturing moments that will never happen again, and knowing people can hold onto those memories through my photos makes me happy.” Tell us about some of the current projects you are working on. Do you have any projects in particular you are looking forward to? Right now, I’m working with a few Indonesian musicians, capturing their moments on stage as usual. I’m also really looking forward to future overseas trips with them. © Martinus Ragita using LomoChrome Purple Pétillant 35 mm ISO 100–400 What’s coming up on the horizon? What’s in the works and what’s on your mind? I’m looking forward to growing more through my work, trying new things, and continuing to capture moments that make people feel something. © Martinus Ragita using Berlin Kino B&W 35 mm ISO 400 Any messages to Lomographers out there? Keep experimenting and find your own colors. Thank you, Martinus, for sharing his photos, knowledge, and experiences with us all! Keep up with his latest work on Instagram and portfolio on his website.
