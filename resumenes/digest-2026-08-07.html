Stage Photographer Martinus Ragita Dives into the World of Lomography

_Music is visual._Martinus Ragita is a professional Indonesian stage and concert photographer who translates the raw energy, rhythm, and atmosphere of live performances into timeless frames. He serves as the primary photographer for several of Indonesia's biggest contemporary music acts, including .Feast, Hindia, Lomba Sihir, and breakout pop star Bernadya. We sent two rolls of the LomoChrome Purple Pétillant 35 mm ISO 100–400 and Berlin Kino B&W 35 mm ISO 400. He wasted no time and dove straight into the world of Lomography.
