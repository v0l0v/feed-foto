Solo Wedding Project: Bird (Film Full Photo) Captures Self-Love with the Lomomatic 110

For Bird, the photographer behind Film Full Photo, shooting on film is more than about documenting a moment; it's a way of telling stories through the photographer's own eye and emotions. She used the Lomomatic 110 to bring her _Solo Wedding_ concept to life, a personal project she created to share the idea that “Happiness doesn't depend on someone else – it begins with ourselves.” Every frame captured with this tiny camera not only preserves a meaningful moment, but also reflects her unique creative vision and heartfelt approach to storytelling.
