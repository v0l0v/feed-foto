‘Bodega Cats of New York’ Advocates for the City’s Beloved Feline Shopkeepers

In 2022, New York resident and bonafide feline devotee Dan Rimada felt that something needed to be done to shine a light on the city's cats. The Big Apple not only has a real feral issue, but many of these creatures make their homes in iconic shops found around the city: bodegas. Over time, locals get to know them and they become fixtures of shelves and countertops. Technically, though, New York City considers it a health code violation to have animals in businesses that serve food. In spite of that, thousands of cats serve as mousers and rat catchers, and a lot of people don't realize that the snoozy tabby nestled atop the Doritos bags is actually illegal.
