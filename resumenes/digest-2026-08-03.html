Collecting Mailbox Memories: Stories from the Lomographers’ Print Exchange

The Community has brought back one of our most cherished analogue traditions: the Lomographers’ Print Exchange. Since then, we have constantly been reminded of the simple yet magical act of sending and receiving photographs to connect one film lover to another. Over the past months, prints have crossed borders across the globe – from Brazil to Australia, Hong Kong to Spain, Poland to the United States, and more. We caught up with a few participants to see how their exchanges went down, inviting them to look back on the prints they sent and the postcards that have since found their way into their mailboxes.
