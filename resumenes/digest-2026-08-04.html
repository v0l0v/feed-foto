Cryptic Photos by Susan kae Grant Plumb the World of Dreams in ‘Night Journey’

A house spins out of control in a wind storm, animals loom large in the imaginations of children, and mysterious creatures lurk in the darkness of Susan kae Grant’s shadow puppet-like photographs. For more than two decades, the artist has plumbed the mind's eye, especially what it sees when we sleep. In the early 1990s, she even began collaborating with a researcher in a sleep laboratory at UT Southwestern Medical Center. Study and documentation of what she visualized in her own REM cycle led to phrases and vocabulary about what she dreamt that she continues to tap into for her studio work.

Fashion Photographer Mario Giovanni's First Impressions of the Diana F+

Mario Giovanni (@mariogvnni) is a professional photographer, videographer, and content creator based in Jakarta and Bali. His visual method focuses on capturing raw emotions, fluid movements, and deep stories in every single frame, heavily focusing on emotional cinematography, dramatic lighting setups, and rich color grading tailored for the fashion, beauty, commercial, and portrait genres. He also frequently steps into the director's chair, producing conceptual and editorial videos. What sets Mario apart is his background in Psychology. This allows him to understand consumer behavior deeply, ensuring his fashion imagery is not only artistic but also commercially impactful. Recently, we sent him the Diana F+ and some medium format film stocks, which he took to his photoshoots.

Through Generations: Grandma Kinso & Granddaughter Yan-Han Preserve Native Language & Family Memories Through Storytelling

Whenever our thoughts turn to the people we love, what stays with us are the little moments and small things that make them smile. But have you ever wondered how they see the world? Maybe the truest way to know someone isn’t through what they say, but through what they see. So we’re passing the camera around, seeing our loved ones through the moments that catch their eyes. Grandma Kinso and her granddaughter Yan-Han run the Instagram account Grandma Kinso's Stories, where Grandma shares stories from her nearly century-long life in the Taiwanese language, while Yan-Han brings these stories to life with her editing and subtitling. Through this approach, the two pass down memories and their mother tongue, as well as leave behind important records of multiple eras.
